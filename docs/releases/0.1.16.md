# ComposeLens 0.1.16

- Adds loss-aware authored and effective support for the expanded [Build family](../typed-model.md),
  including contexts, inputs, caches, Dockerfiles, resource settings, and sensitive SSH forms.
- Expands the native [Deploy model](../typed-model.md) with endpoint/replica settings, lifecycle
  configuration, placement, resource limits/reservations, and schema-only device/resource evidence.
- Adds source-aware service `credential_spec`, `extends`, `provider`, `post_start`, `pre_stop`, `pre_start`, raw
  `runtime`/`pull_refresh_after`/`platform` strings, raw-classified `cgroup` namespaces, and raw
  `cgroup_parent` strings, raw-categorized `cpu_count`/`cpu_percent`/`cpu_period`/`cpu_quota`/`cpu_rt_period` values to
  the authored and effective views; their exact contracts and merge behavior are in the [typed model](../typed-model.md).
- Adds authored, merged, and generated service terminal/security choices and logging, plus generated
  service-network attachments; authored/effective `attach` and non-generated `blkio_config` retain
  source evidence without runtime, provider, CLI, compatibility, controller, I/O, or cross-format
  claims; see the
  [generated-document guide](../generated-rendering.md).
- Expands authored/merged IPAM and generated application-owned network and volume configuration,
  including drivers, options, labels, and conflict diagnostics.
- Remains source-compatible within the 0.1.x policy and supports Rust 1.85.0+; provider and runtime
  behavior remain outside this release's evidence boundary.
